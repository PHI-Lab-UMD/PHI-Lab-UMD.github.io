Welcome to the **Precision Health Intelligence (PHI Φ) Lab** at the University of Maryland School of Public Health, co-directed by [Dr. Tianzhou (Charles) Ma](https://sph.umd.edu/people/tianzhou-ma) and Dr. Menglu (Maggie) Liang.

We develop statistical and machine learning methods to turn complex biomedical data into actionable insight for human health. Our work spans transcriptomic meta-analysis and data integration, Bayesian modelling, causal inference, and high-dimensional variable selection, with applications across neuroscience, cancer, aging and population health.

Much of our current research asks how genetic and lifestyle factors shape brain aging and addiction, drawing on large-scale resources including the UK Biobank, the All of Us Research Program, and ABCD. We are also building methods for population-specific risk prediction, so that models developed in one population transfer fairly to others.

We are grateful for support from the National Institute on Drug Abuse, the National Institute of Mental Health, and the University of Maryland Grand Challenges Grants Program.

**Interested in joining?** We welcome PhD and Master's students at the University of Maryland from Statistics, Biostatistics, Bioinformatics, Computer Science, Applied Mathematics and related fields. Please email Dr. Ma with your CV.
