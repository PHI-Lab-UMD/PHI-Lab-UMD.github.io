## Directors

**Tianzhou (Charles) Ma, PhD** — Co-Director
Associate Professor of Biostatistics, Department of Epidemiology and Biostatistics, University of Maryland. His research develops statistical methods and software in genetics and bioinformatics, with expertise in meta-analysis, data integration, Bayesian analysis, machine learning and high-dimensional variable selection, applied across neuroscience, cancer and epidemiology.
[Faculty page](https://sph.umd.edu/people/tianzhou-ma) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=V8o30rYAAAAJ&hl=en) · tma0929@umd.edu

**Menglu (Maggie) Liang, PhD** — Co-Director
Assistant Clinical Professor of Biostatistics, Department of Epidemiology and Biostatistics, University of Maryland. PhD in Biostatistics from Penn State, MS in Statistics from Minnesota, MS in Epidemiology from Johns Hopkins. Her research covers early detection of cardiovascular disease, dynamic prediction in survival analysis, and Bayesian network meta-analysis.
mliang23@umd.edu

## Prospective students

If you are a PhD or Master's student in Statistics, Biostatistics, Bioinformatics, Computer Science, Applied Mathematics or a related field at the University of Maryland and are interested in our research, please email Dr. Ma with your recent CV. We have ongoing projects in several directions and are open to advising new students, including those looking for summer research opportunities.
